Our laboratories are equipped with the latest technology to conduct high-precision tissue culture, micropropagation, and quality analysis. Each lab is designed to provide a sterile, controlled environment that ensures accuracy and reliability in every propagation cycle.

Production

Cutting-Edge Production Facilities

At Sree Bhairava Nursery, our state-of-the-art production facilities are designed to support large-scale cultivation and propagation of diverse plant species. Our commitment to innovation and sustainability ensures that we produce high-quality plants efficiently and responsibly.

Automated Greenhouse Systems

State-of-the-art automated climate control systems in our greenhouses provide ideal growing conditions year-round, enhancing plant health and productivity during acclimatization.

Custom Growth Chambers

Precision-controlled growth chambers that simulate various environmental conditions, enabling targeted optimization of plant growth parameters for each species.

Rigorous Quality Control

Stringent quality control measures enforced at every stage of production — from initial explant selection to final field-ready delivery — ensuring the highest standards of plant health.

Collaborative R&D Facilities

Collaborative spaces for interdisciplinary research and development, fostering innovation and supporting partnerships with academic and industry leaders in agriculture.

Advanced Analytical Equipment

Cutting-edge analytical tools and equipment for precise measurement and monitoring of plant growth, health, and productivity at every propagation stage.

Customized Production Solutions

Tailored propagation services to meet specific client needs — from small-scale research projects to large-scale commercial operations for diverse crop varieties.
