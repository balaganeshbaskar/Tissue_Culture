Our Experience

Decades of Expertise. Years of Proven Global Partnerships.

Sree Bhairava Nursery brings together more than 15 years of successful business operations and over 40 years of industry leadership in tissue culture technology.

Since our establishment, we have built a strong reputation as a trusted producer and exporter of premium tissue culture plants, serving customers across international markets and supporting the growth of nurseries, distributors, growers, and horticultural enterprises worldwide.

Our company is led by Mr. M. Chidambaram, a highly respected tissue culture professional whose career spans more than four decades in the plant biotechnology industry. Having held leadership roles in some of India’s prominent large-scale tissue culture organizations, he brings unparalleled technical expertise, commercial insight, and industry knowledge to every aspect of our operations.

This combination of experienced leadership, scientific excellence, and export capability enables us to consistently deliver planting material that meets the quality, reliability, and performance expectations of global customers.
